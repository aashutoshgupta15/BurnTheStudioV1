The brief is where a project starts, not where it ends. We take it apart and keep only what is true.

    Burn the brief

    Ship and learn

    Keep going with a managed product team that already knows your problem and your codebase.

What the brief says is rarely the problem.

Three examples of how a request changes once we ask why.

The brief
Build us a ride-hailing app for plumbers.
The problem
Customers wait days for a plumber and never know when one will arrive.
What we would build
A booking and live-arrival MVP for a single city.
The brief
We need an AI chatbot on the website.
The problem
Support spends most of the day answering the same dozen questions.
What we would build
A help flow that answers those and hands off the rest.
The brief
Make us a launch video.
The problem
Buyers do not understand the product in the first ten seconds.
What we would build
A 30-second film and three cut-downs for LinkedIn.
